Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to read ClaimFacts, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or captions. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across this site and our newsletter.

What we do

  • Text is real text, not images of text.
  • Color contrast is checked against WCAG thresholds.
  • Charts that use color also use words, so no meaning depends on color alone.
  • Images carry text descriptions.
  • Our videos carry burned-in captions.

Where we fall short

Some sources we link to, such as journal and regulator pages, are outside our control and may not be accessible. If you cannot get to a source we cite, email us and we will send the relevant passage in an accessible format.

Tell us

If anything here does not work for you, email haris@claimfacts.co and we will fix it or get you the content another way, usually within five business days.

Claim review services — supplement and wellness marketing

This Service Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of __________ (the “Effective Date”) between ClaimFacts (“Provider”) and __________ (“Client”). Provider and Client are each a “Party” and together the “Parties.”

1. Services

Provider will perform scientific and regulatory review of Client’s marketing claims (the “Services”). Client has selected: __________, at __________ __________.

ServiceScopeTurnaround

2. What the Services are not

Provider is not a law firm and does not practice law. The Services do not constitute legal advice, a legal opinion, or a determination of Client’s legal rights, obligations, or liability. No attorney-client relationship is created by this Agreement or by any deliverable produced under it. Client is solely responsible for obtaining legal counsel and for all decisions regarding its marketing, labeling, and regulatory filings.

Provider does not represent Client before the Food and Drug Administration, the Federal Trade Commission, any state authority, or any advertising platform or marketplace. Provider does not conduct clinical studies, does not generate substantiation, and does not verify the authenticity of documents Client supplies.

Where Client sells coaching, programs, courses, or other non-product services, Provider may identify language that presents as clinical diagnosis or treatment. Such findings are observations about how the language reads against clinical and scientific usage. Provider does not advise on professional licensing, scope of practice, or the requirements of any state licensing board, and Client is solely responsible for obtaining legal advice on those questions.

3. Basis of review

Reviews are conducted against publicly available standards including the Federal Trade Commission’s Health Products Compliance Guidance, the structure/function claim provisions of 21 C.F.R. § 101.93, the FTC Endorsement Guides at 16 C.F.R. Part 255, the published advertising and seller policies of platforms and marketplaces identified by Client, and the published scientific literature underlying any physiological or psychological mechanism Client asserts. Provider evaluates whether the substantiation Client supplies supports the claims Client makes, and identifies language that departs from those standards. Where a determination is genuinely ambiguous, Provider will say so and recommend Client consult counsel.

4. Client responsibilities

  • Supply complete and accurate copy for each asset submitted, including headlines, body text, bullets, testimonials, badges, and any on-image text.
  • Supply the substantiation Client relies on for its claims, including studies, supplier documentation, and certificates of analysis, where substantiation review is requested.
  • Identify the platforms and marketplaces on which the copy will run.
  • Make all final decisions regarding whether and how to act on any finding.

Provider’s findings are limited to the materials Client submits. Provider is not responsible for exposure arising from copy, imagery, or substantiation not provided for review.

5. Fees and payment

One-time engagements are invoiced in full upon acceptance and are payable before delivery of the final report unless otherwise agreed in writing. Recurring engagements are invoiced monthly in advance. Invoices are due on receipt and are considered late after seven (7) days, after which Provider may suspend Services until the balance is cleared. All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Fees paid are non-refundable once work on the applicable period or engagement has begun.

6. Term and termination

Recurring engagements run month to month beginning on the Effective Date and renew automatically until cancelled. Either Party may cancel with seven (7) days’ written notice before the next billing date. Cancellation does not entitle Client to a refund of the current period. Either Party may terminate immediately for material breach that remains uncured for ten (10) days after written notice.

7. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other, including unreleased product formulations, marketing plans, substantiation, and the contents of deliverables, and will use it only to perform or receive the Services. This obligation survives for two (2) years after termination. It does not apply to information that is public through no fault of the receiving Party, was already known without obligation, or is required to be disclosed by law.

8. Ownership

Upon payment in full, Client owns the written findings and the replacement copy prepared for Client under this Agreement, and may use them without restriction. Provider retains ownership of its methodology, rubrics, review templates, internal tooling, and the format of the claims library, and may continue to use general knowledge, skills, and experience gained in performing the Services.

The Services are provided on an “as is” basis. Provider makes no warranty, express or implied, that following its findings will prevent regulatory enforcement, warning letters, civil penalties, litigation, advertising account restriction, listing suppression, or any other adverse action. Regulatory standards and platform policies change, are applied inconsistently, and involve judgment on which reasonable professionals differ. Provider disclaims all implied warranties, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.

10. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Provider’s total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not exceed the fees actually paid by Client to Provider in the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither Party will be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, lost revenue, or lost business opportunity, even if advised of the possibility.

11. Indemnification

Client will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider from any third-party claim, demand, proceeding, loss, or expense (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising out of Client’s marketing, labeling, products, or substantiation, or out of Client’s decision to publish, modify, or disregard any finding or replacement copy provided under this Agreement.

12. Independent contractor

Provider performs the Services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship. Provider controls the manner and means of performing the Services and may engage subcontractors, remaining responsible for their work.

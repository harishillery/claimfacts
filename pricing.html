Why we don't charge a percentage of your spend

Some compliance vendors price against media spend. We don't, and the reason matters to you more than it does to us.

Someone who earns more when you spend more has a quiet incentive to flag more, so the fee looks justified. That's the same conflict that makes your performance agency the wrong people to check their own claims. A flat fee keeps the judgment clean. Your spend decides which tier fits — because bigger spend means more copy, shipped faster — not what the work is worth.

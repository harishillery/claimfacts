Privacy Policy

We collect what we need to send you the newsletter and to deliver work you pay for. We do not sell your information, we do not share it for advertising, and we run no advertising trackers on this site.

Who we are

ClaimFacts is a business name of Haris Hillery, a sole proprietor (“ClaimFacts,” “we,” “us”). This policy covers claimfacts.co, The Substantiation File newsletter, our social media accounts, and our paid review services. Questions go to haris@claimfacts.co.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information, such as health conditions, government identifiers or precise location. Please do not send us your own health information. Material submitted for review should be marketing copy, not personal data about your customers.

How we use it

  • To send the newsletter you asked for, and to see which sections readers find useful.
  • To deliver, invoice and support reviews you buy, and to keep business and tax records.
  • To contact businesses about our services, with an opt-out in every message.
  • To keep the site secure and working.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our terms.

We do not make decisions about you by automated means that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act. We have not done so in the past twelve months.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics cookies. Our Cookie Notice has the details, including pages our providers host. Newsletter emails contain standard open and click tracking; you can block open tracking by turning off automatic image loading in your email app.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We do not track visitors across other websites, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a valid request to opt out of any sale or sharing, which we do not do in any case. No third party is permitted to collect information about your activity across sites through this website.

How long we keep it

  • Subscribers: while you are subscribed. When you unsubscribe, we keep your email address on a suppression list only so we never email you again. Ask us to delete it entirely and we will; we then cannot guarantee you stay off the list if the address is added again later.
  • Clients: for the engagement and up to seven years afterward, because tax and business-record law requires it.
  • Server logs: under 30 days, on our hosting provider’s schedule.
  • Business contacts: until you ask us to stop, or twelve months after last contact, whichever comes first.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter in one click, from any issue.
  • Ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Ask us to stop contacting you.

Email haris@claimfacts.co. We answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking. To protect you, we confirm each request by replying to the address we have on file before acting on it. You may use an authorized agent; we will ask the agent for your written permission and confirm with you directly. We keep a record of each request and our answer for 24 months.

California residents. You have the rights above under California law, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. Under California Civil Code § 1798.83 you may ask whether we disclosed personal information to third parties for their direct marketing; we do not.

Residents of the European Economic Area and United Kingdom. We rely on your consent for the newsletter, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing; on performance of a contract for paid reviews; on our legitimate interests for site security and business outreach; and on legal obligation for tax records. You may also object to or restrict processing and receive your data in a portable format. Your information is processed in the United States, and our providers transfer it under recognized safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

We use providers with established security programs, two-factor authentication on every account that holds personal information, and access limited to the one person who runs this business. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you of a breach affecting your information as the law requires.

Children

Our site, newsletter and services are for adults running businesses or creating content. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe we have, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

When this policy changes we update the effective date above. If a change is material, we say so in the next issue and email clients before it takes effect.

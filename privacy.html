Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect your email address so we can send you the newsletter, and we don't sell it, rent it, or hand it to anyone who isn't helping us deliver that email.

Who we are

ClaimFacts is operated by Haris Hillery in the United States. It publishes The Substantiation File, a free weekly newsletter, and runs this website at claimfacts.co. Questions about this policy go to harishillery@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you subscribe: your email address, and any first name you choose to give. Nothing else is required.

When you read the newsletter: our email provider records whether an issue was delivered, whether it was opened, and which links were clicked. This is standard for newsletter platforms and tells us which sections are worth keeping.

When you visit the site: our hosting provider keeps ordinary server logs, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies of its own and runs no third-party trackers.

How we use it

To send you the newsletter, to confirm your subscription, to see in aggregate which content is useful, and to answer you if you write to us. We may occasionally mention ClaimFacts' paid review service at the end of an issue; we will not send you separate marketing email you didn't sign up for.

Who processes it

We use a small number of service providers, each of which handles your information only to provide its service to us:

  • Kit (kit.com) stores the subscriber list and sends the newsletter. Its own privacy policy is at kit.com/privacy.
  • Vercel hosts this website and keeps its server logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, which means your browser requests them from Google.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We would disclose it only if the law required us to.

How long we keep it

Your email address stays on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Unsubscribing stops all newsletter email immediately; if you also want the address removed from our provider's records entirely, write to us and we will do it.

Your choices

Every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can also email us at any time to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and we will respond within a reasonable time, generally thirty days.

Do Not Track. Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no common standard for responding to it, so the site does not change its behavior when it receives one. As described above, the site itself does not track you across other websites.

Children

The newsletter is written for adults working in the supplement and wellness industry. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13, and if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and subscriber data lives with Kit rather than on our own systems. No method of storing or sending data is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect to the minimum the newsletter needs.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page, and if the change is material we will say so in the next issue.

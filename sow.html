2. Scope

Volume cap

— pieces per engagement. A piece means one of the following: a single product detail page; a single sales or landing page; a single email; a single ad creative together with its accompanying copy; a single label or packaging panel; a single marketplace listing; or a single course or program module. A landing page and the ad driving to it are two pieces. Revisions to a piece already reviewed within the same period are not counted again.

For each piece submitted, Provider will deliver written findings identifying language that departs from the standards named in the Master Services Agreement, the specific standard implicated by each finding, a severity assessment, and proposed replacement copy. Where substantiation is supplied, Provider will map each claim to the evidence Client relies on and identify claims the evidence does not support.

Assets submitted beyond the cap will be reviewed at $250 per piece, or held to the following period at Client's election. Work outside this scope requires a separate SOW.
